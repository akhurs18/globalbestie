Before you order

Preorder policy

  • 50% advance required A 50% advance is required to confirm your preorder. The remaining balance is due when your shipment arrives in Pakistan, before local dispatch.
  • Delivery time is approx. 4 weeks Preorders typically take around 4 weeks from acceptance to delivery. Timing can vary depending on the USA shipment batch and courier.
  • Shoe boxes cost extra Shoes are shipped without the original box by default. If you want the box, there is an additional charge of PKR 2,500 — please request this at the time of ordering, not after.
  • Prices are fixed — no discounts All prices are final and already include taxes, customs duties, and international shipping fees. We cannot offer further discounts.
  • Prices are all-inclusive The PKR price on every product is the final amount you'll pay. It already covers sourcing, international shipping, and our service — no surprise add-ons at checkout.
  • Couriers are third-party Once a parcel is dispatched locally, it is handled by a third-party courier. We do not control their timeline. Parcels being dispatched does not mean immediate delivery — please be patient and allow time for local transit.

Ordering & payment

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag, checkout with your name, WhatsApp number, city, address, and notes, then submit the order for team review.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept direct bank deposits. At checkout you pay the 50% advance to confirm your preorder, and we collect the remaining 50% once your parcel arrives in Pakistan, before local dispatch.

When do I pay the remaining preorder balance?

You pay the remaining balance after your shipment arrives in Pakistan and before we dispatch it locally through courier. Your order tracker will show when the shipment reaches this stage.

Is bank transfer safe?

Yes. Your order is reviewed manually, and you can add a transfer reference or upload a confirmation screenshot so our team can match your advance faster.

Can I cancel my order?

You can request cancellation before an order is accepted or sourced. Once a USA preorder has been purchased, cancellation depends on the brand or retailer policy.

Do you offer refunds or returns?

Refunds and returns depend on order stage, product type, and the USA retailer policy. We review issues case by case before sourcing and after delivery.

Is the PKR price on the site the final price?

Yes. Every product is listed in PKR with everything included — sourcing, international shipping, and our service. No conversion math, no surprise add-ons at checkout.

Shipping & products

How long does delivery take?

Preorder delivery takes approximately 4 weeks. In-stock products move faster after payment review. Timing can shift because USA shipments are consolidated in batches, and couriers are third-party — we confirm the current batch ETA when your order is accepted.

Are customs or duties included?

Our quote includes the working shipping estimate shown at checkout. If a product needs unusual duty handling because of size, weight, or category, our team confirms before accepting the order.

Do you deliver to my city?

Yes. We deliver to major cities across Pakistan including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, and more. WhatsApp us if you are unsure about your area.

Are your products authentic?

We source from USA brand stores, official retailers, or trusted retail channels. Product pages and order notes can include source preferences where needed.

Do shoes come with the original box?

Shoes are shipped without the original box by default. If you want the box, there is an additional charge of PKR 2,500. You must request this at the time of ordering — we cannot arrange it after the order has been placed.

Can I request a specific product?

Yes. Send the product link, size, shade, or screenshot through order notes or WhatsApp and the team will quote you a final PKR price.
